One graph object from every method

A method returns a StructureOutput whose structure is a GraphStructure: the graph type, a mark at each end of every edge, and the variable names. Draw it, convert it to NetworkX or a NumPy matrix, or compare it with another method's graph, with the same code for every method. Time-series methods return a graph over time lags.

Three methods, three answers

The sprinkler network: season drives rain and the sprinkler, both wet the grass, and wet grass is slippery. The methods agree on everything downstream of season, and differ on the two edges out of it.
