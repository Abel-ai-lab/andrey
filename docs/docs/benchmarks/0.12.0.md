# 0.12.0 release card: Inter-IAMB, MMHC, and RSMAX2

Inter-IAMB, MMHC, and RSMAX2 are supported methods. We compare each with bnlearn 5.2.1 on the
graph it returns, scored against that graph's own truth: the CPDAG for MMHC and RSMAX2 and for
Inter-IAMB's default `structure="cpdag"`, the skeleton for `structure="skeleton"`, and the moral
graph for `structure="moral"`, which bnlearn's `learn.mb` blankets joined by the AND rule give.

A setting is one size, sample size, and mean degree. D is Andrey's error minus bnlearn's on the
same dataset. A setting with at least 10 paired datasets is worse when the paired 95% bootstrap
interval of the mean D lies wholly above 0, better when wholly below, and a tie otherwise. The
time ratio is bnlearn's fit time over Andrey's, the median of the settings' median ratios. In no
setting does bnlearn make significantly fewer errors.

## Method

**Data.** Linear-Gaussian data from Erdos-Renyi graphs, standardized, with 20, 50, and 100
variables, 1,000 and 5,000 rows, and mean degree 2 and 4. Each setting has up to 20 datasets, one
per seed, with the columns in a shuffled order; every setting keeps at least 10 paired datasets.

**Fits.** One fit at a time on one core, Fisher-z tests at alpha 0.05 (bnlearn `test="zf"`) and the
Gaussian BIC for the score phase (bnlearn `score="bic-g"`), a 600 s cap per fit, and one untimed
warm-up fit except at 5,000 rows, mean degree 4, and 50 or more variables, where there is none. A
fit that reaches the cap is a timeout. It is counted as coverage, left out of the pairs, and never
scored as an empty graph. The Andrey fits all finished; the timeouts are bnlearn's.

**Errors.** The structural Hamming distance of the returned graph to its own truth, averaged over
the paired datasets; a DAG that bnlearn returns is compared through its CPDAG.

## Limits

The settings cover 20 to 100 variables and 1,000 to 5,000 rows on linear-Gaussian data with
Fisher-z tests. Larger graphs, other data types, and other tests are not measured. bnlearn reached
the 600 s cap in some settings at 5,000 rows and mean degree 4, so those settings have fewer paired
datasets; each table lists the datasets it pairs. At 20 variables and mean degree 4, MMHC and
RSMAX2 make 29 to 31 errors against 40 for the empty graph, the closest of any setting.

## Reproduce

You can find environment setup, campaign drivers, and scores in the [benchmark harness
guide][harness]. To rerun the comparison, install Andrey 0.12.0 (andrey-core) and bnlearn 5.2.1 with
R 4.6.1 in the harness's R environment. Every driver shuffles the columns by default. Times will
differ with the hardware and build; graphs should match closely.
