# 0.13.0 release card: IAMB

IAMB is a supported method. We compare it with bnlearn 5.2.1 on the graph it returns, scored against
that graph's own truth: the CPDAG for the default `structure="cpdag"`, the skeleton for
`structure="skeleton"`, and the moral graph for `structure="moral"`, which bnlearn's `learn.mb`
blankets joined by the AND rule give. Three grids of linear-Gaussian data measure it: standardized
columns, columns left in their own units, and 200 variables.

A setting is one size, sample size, and mean degree. D is Andrey's error minus bnlearn's on the same
dataset. A setting with at least 10 paired datasets is worse when the paired 95% bootstrap interval
of the mean D lies wholly above 0, better when wholly below, and a tie otherwise; a setting with
fewer is listed, not judged. A worse setting counts as worse only if a rerun on fresh seeds is worse
too, so the counts above treat one that is not as a tie. The time ratio is bnlearn's fit time over
Andrey's, the median of the settings' median ratios. The worst setting is the one with the largest D
from the first run.

## Where bnlearn makes fewer errors

3 of the 82 settings are worse in their first run:

| Grid | Setting | Graph | Andrey / bnlearn (mean errors) | D, first run [95%] | Rerun on fresh seeds: D [95%] | Counts as |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| standardized | d 50, n 5,000, degree 2 | CPDAG | 9.15 / 8.65 | +0.50 [+0.05, +1.05] | not rerun; cause traced below | worse |
| unstandardized | d 20, n 1,000, degree 4 | CPDAG | 22.10 / 21.50 | +0.60 [+0.10, +1.10] | -0.10 [-0.30, +0.00] (10 datasets, seeds 20-29) | tie |
| unstandardized | d 20, n 5,000, degree 4 | CPDAG | 17.10 / 16.20 | +0.90 [+0.40, +1.50] | -0.10 [-0.30, +0.00] (10 datasets, seeds 20-29) | tie |

The two unstandardized settings at 20 variables and mean degree 4 do not replicate: the rerun's D is
-0.10 on both, and 7 and 9 of its 10 graphs are identical. The standardized setting with 50
variables, 5,000 rows, and mean degree 2 is worse (16 of its 20 graphs are identical). Most of its
gap, +7 pairs of the +10 over its 20 datasets, sits in 2 datasets whose skeletons differ, and the
skeleton difference starts at one node's first grow step, where the p-values of several candidates
underflow to exactly 0 in bnlearn, which takes the first column among them. Andrey takes the
candidate with the strongest test statistic, the association measure of Tsamardinos, Aliferis, and
Statnikov (2003), so its result does not depend on the column order. The choice bnlearn makes,
applied in Andrey's search, reproduces bnlearn's skeleton in all 4 traced datasets. The other +3 are
orientation differences in 2 datasets with equal skeletons: Meek's rules on a cyclic partially
directed graph, and a collider that bnlearn skips for closing a directed cycle. The skeleton of the
same cell is a tie, and so is its CPDAG on unstandardized data (-0.20 [-0.60, +0.00]).

## Method

**Data.** Linear-Gaussian data from Erdos-Renyi graphs with 20, 50, and 100 variables, 1,000 and
5,000 rows, and mean degree 2 and 4, as standardized columns and as columns left in their own units,
and with 200 variables, 1,000 and 5,000 rows, and mean degree 2 and 4, on the outputs whose fits at
100 variables all finished. Each setting has up to 20 datasets (10 at 200 variables), one per seed,
with the columns in a shuffled order. The CPU budget stopped some settings at 10 to 16 datasets. A
setting keeps at least 10, and the ones that bnlearn could not finish keep fewer pairs.

**Fits.** One fit at a time on one core, Fisher-z tests at alpha 0.05 (bnlearn `test="zf"`), a 600 s
cap per fit, and one untimed warm-up fit except at 5,000 rows, mean degree 4, and 50 or more
variables, where there is none. A fit that reaches the cap is a timeout. It is counted as coverage,
left out of the pairs, and never scored as an empty graph.

**Errors.** The structural Hamming distance of the returned graph to its own truth. The tables give
the median over the paired datasets, for Andrey, bnlearn, and the empty graph; D is the mean of the
per-dataset differences, the statistic of the verdict. A DAG that bnlearn returns is compared
through its CPDAG.

**Rerun.** A worse setting is rerun once on 10 fresh seeds (20 to 29), with the same build and arms.
The verdict of the rerun is its own interval.

## Speed

Andrey is faster in the median on every grid: 3.8x on standardized data, 2.1x on unstandardized
data, and 2.1x at 200 variables, each the median over the grid's settings and three graphs. The
ratio of a setting is the median of the per-dataset ratios of the two fit times on one core. 4 of
the 82 settings are slower than bnlearn, none by more than 15%: moral graph at d 20, n 1,000, degree
2 (0.88, standardized); moral graph at d 50, n 1,000, degree 2 (0.98, standardized); CPDAG at d 100,
n 1,000, degree 2 (0.93, unstandardized); moral graph at d 20, n 1,000, degree 4 (0.85,
unstandardized).

## Limits

The settings cover 20 to 200 variables and 1,000 to 5,000 rows on linear-Gaussian data with Fisher-z
tests. Other data types and other tests are not measured here. At 100 variables, 1,000 rows, and
mean degree 2 on unstandardized data, the mean CPDAG of Andrey makes 79 errors against 100 for the
empty graph, the closest of any setting (bnlearn: 80); on standardized data it is 20 variables,
1,000 rows, and mean degree 4, with 24 against 40.

Two CPDAG settings have 9 paired datasets and are not judged: 100 variables, 5,000 rows, and mean
degree 4 on standardized data, where bnlearn reached the cap on 11 datasets, and 200 variables,
1,000 rows, and mean degree 4, where it reached the cap on 1. Andrey finished every fit. Over all
settings bnlearn reached the cap on 19 standardized-data fits (12 CPDAG, 7 skeleton) and 1 fit at
200 variables. At 200 variables the skeleton and CPDAG of 5,000 rows and mean degree 4 are not
measured, because bnlearn did not finish all fits at 100 variables there.

## Reproduce

You can find environment setup, campaign drivers, and scores in the [benchmark harness
guide][harness]. To rerun the comparison, install Andrey (andrey-core) and bnlearn 5.2.1 with R
4.6.1 in the harness's R environment. Every driver shuffles the columns by default. Times will
differ with the hardware and build; graphs should match closely.
