## Where the time went

TODO: the ideas behind the speedups, one short part each, linking the "Why Andrey is fast" post
where an idea is already explained:

- Constraint-based searches: skeleton queries prepared once, batched by level, and spread over
  worker processes.
- Score-based searches: GES and XGES bookkeeping without graph copies; exact search scoring.
- Permutation-based searches: faster BOSS and GRaSP traces.
- LiNGAM and pairwise methods: DirectLiNGAM's peel loop, ICA-LiNGAM's order search, ANM's
  distance matrix, and PNL's batched fits on the CPU and a GPU.
- Constraint-based searches with hidden common causes: FCI's reach sets and GFCI's adjacency lists.
- Memory: first-order queries streamed instead of held at once.
