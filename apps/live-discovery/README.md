## Andrey

Causal discovery, fast. Over 100x faster on PC, and faster on most other supported methods.

Compare live PC, FCI, and DirectLiNGAM fits with Andrey and the other installed packages.
Explore a preset or numeric CSV, read the controlled benchmarks, and step through PC, Meek's
rules, and d-separation.

Live fits run sequentially with one numeric thread on two shared vCPUs. Each fit has a
60-second limit. Controlled benchmark ratios apply to the hardware and datasets used in those
recorded runs.

This alpha Space is private. Its repository includes Andrey's source.
